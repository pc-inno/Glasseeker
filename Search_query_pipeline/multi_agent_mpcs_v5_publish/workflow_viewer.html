Recursive Local-Constraint Workflow

The pipeline starts from an obscure seed target, builds ambiguous evidence paths whose intersection uniquely identifies the target, recursively hides intermediate entities, writes a natural question, and filters the result with independent solver rollouts and an official BrowseComp-style answer judge.

Compositional ambiguity Core clues remain individually underspecified; uniqueness comes from their intersection.
Recursive hiding Intermediate entities inside clues are replaced by their own local constraint trees.
External solvability check Independent solver rollouts and a BrowseComp-style judge estimate difficulty.
Quality control Failures trigger constraint revision, question revision, rejection, or manual review.
